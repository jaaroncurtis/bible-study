# The Fall

**Romans 1:21b-32**

## Aim

Trace what the seizure of the royal prerogative actually did to us, and what
God's response to it is.

This is the lesson that goes into the prophets, because Paul is already there: the
idolatry indictment of Romans 1:21b-23 is built out of theirs.

## Content

- **The declaration of war.** In taking the knowledge of good and evil, we took God's
  place in defining ought-ness. We set out to define our own righteousness.
- **The new master.** We were made to serve. Having refused the true Master, we did
  not become free — we needed another, and the only one on offer was the broken,
  perverted definition of righteousness we ourselves produced. Broken because we were
  never built with the capacity to define it.
- **This is sin**: our own righteousness apart from God. Not merely bad acts — a rival
  standard, and a master.
- **The judgment is the handover.** God "gives us over." The sentence is being left to
  the master we chose.
- **Futility as curse.** Unable to be what we were made to be, we are futile.
- **Reduced providence.** Having rejected God, we are cursed with less of His
  provision, thrown back to some real degree on our own effort to sustain ourselves.
- *Someone will ask why God made a world this was possible in. Do not answer it yet —
  say it is the right question and that [The Golden Chain](lesson:23) is where the letter answers it.*

## Cross-references

### The prophetic material, because Paul's language here is quoted from it

- **Psalm 106:19-20** and **Jeremiah 2:11** — exchanging glory for an image. Paul's
  verb in 1:23 is theirs; this is the charge he is re-filing against everyone.
- **Jeremiah 2:5** — they went after worthlessness and became worthless. Futility as
  the prophets state it: you become like what you serve.
- **Psalm 115:4-8** and **Psalm 135:15-18** — idols with eyes that do not see, and
  makers who become like them. The mechanism of futility, stated plainly.
- **Isaiah 44:9-20** — half the log burned for warmth, half carved into a god. The
  fullest exposure of what the exchange actually is.
- **Jeremiah 2:13** — the two evils: forsaking the fountain, and hewing cisterns that
  hold no water. Reduced providence in the prophets' own image.

### For the seizure itself

- **Genesis 3:1-7** — carried over from [Honor Him as God](lesson:2), now read for what it produced.
- **Genesis 3:16-19** — the ground cursed, thorns, sweat. Where reduced providence
  comes from; Romans 1 gives the handover, Genesis gives the conditions.

## Reflection

- Where am I still trying to define righteousness for myself?
- Can I name a place where being "given over" to my own standard has felt like
  freedom?
- Where do I feel the reduced providence — and how do I usually respond to it?

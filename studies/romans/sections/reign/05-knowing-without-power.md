# Knowing Without Power

**Romans 7:14-25**

## Aim

Expose the insufficiency of knowledge and agreement on their own — the
*notitia* and *assensus* of a man with no power behind them.

## Content

- I do not do what I want, and I do the very thing I hate.
- Agreeing that the law is good while being unable to keep it — assent without
  ability.
- The war between the law of the mind and the law of sin in the members.
- "Who will deliver me?" — the cry the whole chapter exists to produce, and its
  answer.

## Reflection

- Where am I relying on knowing better to make me do better?
- Have I ever asked the question of verse 24 as a real question?

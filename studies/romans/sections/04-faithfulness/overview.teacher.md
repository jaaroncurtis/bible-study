# The Faithfulness of God - teacher notes

**Romans 9:1-11:36**

Notes for whoever is leading, covering the whole section. Not part of the
self-directed study.

### Announce the change of reading

- Six lessons have trained readers to hear Paul's "Jew" as the religious insider and
  to apply it to themselves. That was right for chapters 2-4 and is wrong here.
- In a group, say so explicitly before opening chapter 9. Untraining a reading habit
  does not happen quietly, and someone who carries the cipher in will misread the
  hardest section of the letter and then argue from the misreading.
- The section overview states the distinction to hold: Israel still means Israel, and
  precisely because it is a real people it is the worked example of visible and
  invisible church.

### Where the group will push back

- **Chapter 9 feels unjust.** Expect this, and do not defuse it early. Paul does not
  defuse it either; he sharpens it and then answers with the potter. A group that
  never feels the objection has not read the chapter.
- **Someone will universalise 11:29** into a promise about anyone who once professed
  faith. The lesson on Unsearchable handles it: the branches broken off were in the
  visible community, and the calling that cannot be revoked is the effectual call.
- **Someone will collapse Israel into the church.** The cost is concrete, and it is
  worth naming out loud: Paul's grief in 9:1-3 turns theatrical, 11:1 stops being a
  real question, and the olive tree has no natural branches to break off.

### Pace

This is the section most likely to stall. If time is short, protect the potter and
the olive tree; the two of them carry the argument, and the rest can be summarised.

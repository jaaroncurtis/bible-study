# Unsearchable

**Romans 11:25-36**

## Aim

End the doctrinal half where Paul ends it — in worship rather than
resolution.

## Content

- The partial hardening, until the fullness of the Gentiles comes in.
- **And so all Israel will be saved** (11:26). Israel here is the nation, the same
  people whose hardening Paul has just described. The hardening is partial and it has
  an end: when the fullness of the Gentiles has come in, the people as a people turn
  to their Deliverer. God is not finished with Israel.
- **As regards the gospel they are enemies; as regards election they are beloved,
  for the sake of the fathers** (11:28). One people, both sentences. The same nation
  that stands outside the gospel is the nation God set His love on, and has not let
  go of.
- **The gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable** (11:29). God bound Himself to
  this people, and centuries of unbelief have not undone it.
- **This is not where perseverance is taught; it is where it is corroborated.** The
  doctrine was laid down in [The Golden Chain](lesson:23) from 8:29-30, stated of
  persons. Here the same faithfulness is seen from outside, worked out on a nation
  over centuries: a second witness, from history, to the character 8:29-30 already
  established.
- **Branches broken off, and a calling that stands.** Branches were broken off in
  [Do Not Boast](lesson:30); here the calling is irrevocable. Both are true of Israel
  at once. Individual Israelites fell away in unbelief, and the nation's calling did
  not fall with them: God kept a remnant in every generation, and He will bring the
  people home. That is the visible and the invisible church worked out on a nation.
- Read [The Golden Chain](lesson:23) again alongside this. 8:29-30 is the promise;
  Israel is the evidence that God keeps that kind of promise.
- God consigning all to disobedience that He may have mercy on all.
- The doxology: the depth of His riches and wisdom, His unsearchable judgments.
- "Who has known the mind of the Lord?" — the royal prerogative confessed rather than
  contested. It is the question [The Potter](lesson:26) raised, now asked in worship
  instead of protest.
- From Him and through Him and to Him are all things.

## Cross-references

- **Romans 8:29-30** — carried back from [The Golden Chain](lesson:23). The chain
  applied to persons; the ground for perseverance, with 11:29 as its example in
  history.
- **Romans 9:6** — the distinction this whole section runs on, restated now that the
  argument has landed.
- **Romans 11:5** — the remnant, as the visible/invisible distinction with a number
  attached.
- **Jeremiah 31:35-37** — the fixed order of sun, moon and stars as the measure of how
  long Israel remains a people before God.
- **Zechariah 12:10** — the people looking on the One they pierced, and mourning.

## Reflection

- Can I let a doctrine end in worship rather than in a settled position?
- God kept faith with a people who did not keep faith with Him. What does that tell me
  about the One who holds the chain in 8:29-30?

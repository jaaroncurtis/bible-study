# Paul's Errand

**Romans 15:14-33**

## Aim

See why this letter exists, and what apostolic ambition looks like.

## Content

- Paul's boldness on some points by way of reminder, because of the grace given him.
- A priestly service of the gospel; the offering of the Gentiles.
- Ambition to preach where Christ has not been named.
- Spain, Jerusalem, the collection from Macedonia and Achaia.
- His request for their striving together with him in prayer.

## Reflection

- Do I have any ambition shaped by the gospel rather than by preference?

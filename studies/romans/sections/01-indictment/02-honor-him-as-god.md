# Honor Him as God

**Romans 1:18-21a**

## Aim

Establish who God is and what honoring Him _as God_ requires, because
everything the letter later argues about righteousness rests on it.

## Content

- What do we even mean by "god" in the abstract? And on what grounds is Jehovah _the
  one true God_ rather than one candidate among them?
- The tree of the knowledge of good and evil as God's **royal prerogative** — His
  exclusive right to define good and evil.
- Man created as **vassal king**: real rule, real authority, held under total
  allegiance.
- **Righteousness as ought-ness.** Righteous is being what we were made to be;
  unrighteous is deviating from it.
- Therefore honoring God as God is the _prerequisite_ to righteousness, and it means
  total submission to His definition of ought-ness.
- We are fully righteous once we are, by grace, given back the post we abandoned, and
  given it with a son's standing: loyal vassal king under the King of Kings.
- Chapter 3 will show righteousness _credited_ to people who are not yet what
  they ought to be, because Christ was. The definition stays the same the whole way
  through; what changes is who meets it. Chapter 8 shows what the credit opens
  into: [From Slave to Son to Heir](lesson:20), where the post comes back as a
  son's, not a servant's who earned his way back in.

## Cross-references

- **Genesis 1:26-28** — the commission that makes man a ruler in the first place.
- **Genesis 2:15-17** — the one withheld tree; rule granted, prerogative retained.
- **Luke 3:38** — Adam, the son of God. The vassal king's post was a son's post
  from the start.
- **Genesis 3:1-7** — the seizure, read here for what it claims rather than for its
  consequences, which [The Fall](lesson:3) takes up.

## Reflection

- Whose definition of good and evil am I actually operating on this week?
- Where do I want the authority of a king without the allegiance of a vassal?

# The *Doulos* and the Gospel

**Romans 1:1-17**

## Aim

Put the study's governing words on the table, and notice what those words are
already doing in your thinking.

Deliberately open-ended. Nothing is being settled here. Sit with the questions
rather than answering them quickly.

## Content

- Paul's self-designation. What *doulos* could mean across the ancient Greco-Roman
  world Paul wrote into — the full range available to his first readers, not a
  single dictionary gloss.
- How each of those possible senses changes what Paul is claiming about himself.
- Paul's "obligations" — what he says he owes, and to whom.
- The gospel as Paul states it here: an announcement about a person, not first a
  procedure for being saved.

## Reflection

### On wearing the word himself

- How does it feel to apply *doulos* to myself?
- What do I like about it? What bothers me?
- Where does my actual thinking and decision-making line up with this, and where
  does it quietly deviate?

### On three words the letter runs on — **gospel**, **righteousness**, **faith**

- What do I understand each to mean?
- What does each actually mean to me day to day, as opposed to on paper?
- How do those working definitions shape what I decide?

### Drawing it together

- Can I see a connection between being a *doulos* of Christ and what I mean by
  gospel, righteousness, and faith?

# The Household

**Romans 16:1-16**

## Aim

Read the names as the people they were; doctrine this heavy was addressed to
actual people.

## Content

- Phoebe commended, a servant of the church and a patron of many.
- Prisca and Aquila, who risked their necks; the church in their house.
- The sheer length of the list, and how many of them are women, relatives, fellow
  prisoners, the beloved.
- Greet one another with a holy kiss.

## Reflection

- Would my name appear on someone's list like this? Whose would appear on mine?

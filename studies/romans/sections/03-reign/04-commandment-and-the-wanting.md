# The Commandment and the Wanting

**Romans 7:7-13**

## Aim

Show the law's diagnostic function, and see Genesis 3 replay in the first
person.

## Content

- Is the law sin? By no means — but Paul would not have known coveting apart from it.
- **Epithymia**: the interior wanting that precedes the reaching.
- The sequence — prohibition, desire aroused by the prohibition, transgression, death
  — is the Genesis 3 pattern retold in the first person. The "see, desire, take" shape
  is visible in it.
- Sin seizing its opportunity *through* the commandment: the standard did not create
  the corruption, it made it show itself.
- The law is holy, righteous and good. The verdict falls on us.

## Reflection

- Where does a prohibition make me want the thing more? What does that reveal about
  who is actually in charge?

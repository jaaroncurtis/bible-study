# From Slave to Son to Heir

**Romans 8:12-17**

## Aim

Follow the trajectory past ownership into sonship and inheritance — without
losing either end of it.

## Content

- Led by the Spirit of God: these are sons of God.
- You did not receive a spirit of slavery to fall back into fear, but of adoption.
- Paul's own qualification of the slave language — the same move John 15:15 makes.
  Hold this alongside chapter 6 rather than letting either cancel the other.
- *Abba*, and the Spirit's witness with our spirit.
- Heirs of God, fellow heirs with Christ — the purchased slave now standing to
  inherit.

## Reflection

- Do I relate to God as owned, as adopted, or as an heir — and can all three be true
  at once?
- What fear still operates in me that adoption should have ended?

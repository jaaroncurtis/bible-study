# From Slave to Son to Heir

**Romans 8:12-17**

## Aim

Follow the trajectory past ownership into sonship and inheritance — without
losing either end of it — and see where righteousness was always headed.

## Content

- Led by the Spirit of God: these are sons of God.
- You did not receive a spirit of slavery to fall back into fear, but of adoption.
- Paul's own qualification of the slave language — the same move John 15:15 makes.
  Hold this alongside chapter 6 rather than letting either cancel the other.
- *Abba*, and the Spirit's witness with our spirit.
- Heirs of God, fellow heirs with Christ — the purchased slave now standing to
  inherit.

### Where righteousness arrives

- [Honor Him as God](lesson:2) defined righteousness as being what we were made to
  be, and put us in the post of vassal king. Adoption is how that post comes back.
  Not as servants who earned their way back into the court, but as sons received
  into the house — and a son's standing does not rise and fall with his
  performance on a given day.
- Credited righteousness ([Abraham Credited](lesson:10)) is the legal ground;
  adoption is the relationship the verdict opens into. The Judge who acquits is the
  Father who takes us home.
- Sonship is also the ought-ness itself. Adam was the son of God; the image was
  always a son's likeness. To be what we were made to be is to bear the Father's
  likeness, and the Spirit of adoption is the one forming it.
- So the definition never changed. What changed is that a son can grow into what
  he already is.

## Cross-references

- **Galatians 4:4-7** — sent to redeem, so that we might receive adoption; no
  longer a slave but a son, and if a son then an heir. Romans 8 in miniature.
- **Ephesians 1:5** — predestined in love for adoption. Sonship was the point of
  the choosing.
- **John 1:12-13** — the right to become children of God, born of God and not
  of the will of man.
- **1 John 3:1-2** — what kind of love, that we should be called children of
  God; and when He appears we shall be like Him. Sonship now, likeness finished
  later.
- **Luke 3:38** — Adam, the son of God. The post restored is the post first
  given.
- **Luke 15:17-24** — the son who rehearses asking to be a hired servant, and
  the father who will not hear it. The post given back as a son's.

## Reflection

- Do I relate to God as owned, as adopted, or as an heir — and can all three be true
  at once?
- What fear still operates in me that adoption should have ended?
- Am I still trying to earn back a post that has already been given back to me as a
  son?

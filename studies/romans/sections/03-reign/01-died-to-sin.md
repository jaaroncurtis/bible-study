# Died to Sin

**Romans 6:1-14**

## Aim

Answer the first objection to grace by showing that the believer's
relationship to sin has changed in kind, not in degree.

## Content

- Shall we continue in sin that grace may abound? The question grace always provokes.
- Baptised into His death, buried with Him, raised to walk in newness of life.
- The old self crucified so that the body of sin might be brought to nothing.
- "Consider yourselves dead to sin" — reckoning as acting on what is already true.
- Present your members to God; sin shall not have dominion, for you are not under law
  but under grace.

## Reflection

- Where am I fighting sin as a subject of it rather than as someone transferred out
  from under it?

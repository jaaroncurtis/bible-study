# Released to Another

**Romans 7:1-6**

## Aim

Explain the legal mechanism of the transfer, so freedom from law is not
mistaken for lawlessness.

## Content

- The law binds a person as long as he lives; the marriage analogy.
- Death dissolves the claim — you have died to the law through the body of Christ.
- Released in order to belong to another, to bear fruit for God.
- Serving in the new way of the Spirit, not the old way of the written code.

## Reflection

- Released *to whom*? Do I treat freedom as a destination or as a transfer?

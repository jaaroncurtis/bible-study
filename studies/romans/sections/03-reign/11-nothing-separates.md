# Nothing Separates

**Romans 8:31-39**

## Aim

Take the first eight chapters as assurance before Paul turns to Israel.

## Content

- If God is for us, who can be against us?
- He who did not spare His own Son — the argument from the greater gift to every
  lesser one.
- Who shall bring a charge? Who condemns? Christ interceding.
- The list of things that cannot separate, and the deliberate exhaustiveness of it.

## Reflection

- What do I secretly believe could separate me?
- Is my assurance resting on my grip on Christ or His on me?

# Which Master?

**Romans 6:15-23**

## Aim

The centre of the *doulos* thread: establish that the choice is never
whether to be owned.

## Content

- Paul's premise: you present yourselves to someone, and whoever that is owns you.
- Therefore autonomy is not one of the options. The only live question is which
  master.
- Slaves of sin leading to death, or slaves of obedience leading to righteousness.
- Freedom here is **transfer**, not self-determination.
- Paul's own aside that he speaks in human terms because of the weakness of their
  flesh — the apostle marking the limits of his own metaphor. Worth weighing against
  the temptation to press "slave" flatly everywhere.
- The wage of sin versus the free gift of God — payment against present.

## Reflection

- I present myself to something daily. To what?
- Which of my "free choices" are actually the preferences of a master I did not
  choose?

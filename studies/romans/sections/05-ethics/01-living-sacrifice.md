# The Living Sacrifice

**Romans 12:1-2**

## Aim

Work the hinge of the letter, where everything owed is turned into everything
offered.

## Content

- "Therefore, by the mercies of God" — the ethics run on the doctrine, in that order.
- Present your bodies as a living sacrifice: the same verb as Romans 6, now aimed
  where it belongs.
- Your spiritual, reasonable service — the word is worship and it is also duty.
- Do not be conformed; be transformed by the renewal of the mind.
- Testing and discerning the will of God: **the reclaimed vassal king learning the
  King's definition of ought-ness instead of issuing his own.** This is [Honor Him as God](lesson:2)
  arriving as practice.

## Reflection

- What is currently forming my mind, and did I choose it?
- Where do I still want to discern God's will as an advisor rather than receive it as
  a subject?

# Bear with the Failings

**Romans 15:1-13**

## Aim

Close the ethical section with Christ as the pattern, and Jew and Gentile
worshipping together.

## Content

- The strong ought to bear the failings of the weak, not to please themselves.
- Christ did not please Himself — the pattern is His, not a strategy.
- The Scriptures written for our instruction, that we might have hope.
- Christ a servant to the circumcised to confirm the promises, and the Gentiles
  glorifying God for His mercy.
- The chain of Old Testament quotations, and the God of hope filling with joy and
  peace.

## Reflection

- Am I using my strength to carry someone or to justify myself?

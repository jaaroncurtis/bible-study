# Sober Judgment

**Romans 12:3-8**

## Aim

Apply the renewed mind first to self-assessment.

## Content

- Do not think of yourself more highly than you ought — the measure of faith God has
  assigned.
- One body, many members, and members of one another.
- Gifts differing according to grace: use them.

## Reflection

- Do I know my actual measure, or an inflated and a deflated version I switch between?

# Love Fulfils the Law

**Romans 13:8-14**

## Aim

Bring the law back as fulfilled rather than reimposed, and set the clock
running.

## Content

- Owe nothing except to love; love as the fulfilling of the law.
- The commandments summed up in one.
- Salvation nearer than when we believed; the night far gone.
- Cast off works of darkness, put on the armour of light.
- Put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh to gratify its
  desires — note *epithymia* returning from [The Commandment and the Wanting](lesson:17), now with an answer.

## Reflection

- What provision am I currently making for something I claim to have renounced?

# The Stumbling Stone

**Romans 9:30-10:4**

## Aim

Name precisely what went wrong, in the study's own vocabulary.

## Content

- Gentiles who did not pursue righteousness attained it; Israel pursuing a law of
  righteousness did not arrive.
- Why? Because they pursued it as though by works.
- Zeal for God, but not according to knowledge.
- Being ignorant of the righteousness of God, they sought to establish their own —
  this is **sin as defined in [The Fall](lesson:3)**, in its most religious possible form.
- Christ as the end of the law for righteousness to everyone who believes.

## Reflection

- Where is my zeal running ahead of my knowledge?
- What is my most respectable attempt to establish my own righteousness?

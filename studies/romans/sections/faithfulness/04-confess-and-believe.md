# Confess and Believe

**Romans 10:5-17**

## Aim

Hold together the confession of lordship and the belief of the heart, and
see how faith arrives.

## Content

- The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart.
- Confessing Jesus as **Lord** and believing God raised Him — allegiance and trust in
  one breath, not two separate requirements.
- The same Lord over all, bestowing riches on all who call.
- The chain backwards: calling, believing, hearing, preaching, sending.
- Faith comes by hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ.

## Reflection

- Do I treat "Lord" as a title or as a claim on my decisions?
- What am I listening to most, and what faith is it producing?

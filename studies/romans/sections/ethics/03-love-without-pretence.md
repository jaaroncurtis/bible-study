# Love Without Pretence

**Romans 12:9-21**

## Aim

Work the texture of ordinary Christian conduct, and hand back the bench.

## Content

- Love genuine, evil abhorred, good held fast — rapid-fire imperatives, worth slowing
  down.
- Bless those who persecute; weep and rejoice with others; associate with the lowly.
- Never avenge yourselves — leave it to the wrath of God.
- **Vengeance as an abdicated bench**: refusing revenge is refusing to occupy the
  judgment seat we seized in Genesis 3. "Vengeance is mine" is the royal prerogative
  again, and here obedience means handing it back.
- Overcome evil with good.

## Reflection

- Where am I still sitting on a bench that is not mine?
- Who am I currently judging in place of God?

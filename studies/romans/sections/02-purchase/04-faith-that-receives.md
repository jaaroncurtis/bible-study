# The Faith That Receives

**Romans 4:13-25**

## Aim

Take Abraham's faith apart, and name its parts.

## Content

- The promise comes through the righteousness of faith, not through the law.
- Abraham's faith examined through **notitia** (he knew what was promised),
  **assensus** (he judged God able), and **fiducia** (he rested his life on it).
- Faith as the empty hand: it contributes nothing and receives everything.
- Fully convinced God was able to do what He promised — and this written for our sake
  too.

## Reflection

- Which of the three do I have? Which am I missing?
- Where do knowledge and agreement sit in me without any resting on them?

# Adversarial review findings

## 8. Election is relational, not decretal

- **sections/04-faithfulness/02-potter.md, "fore-loved — these persons as they actually would be, and decreed their existence anyway"** — "decreed" appears in a learner-facing lesson, which the rule forbids outright. It also claims the golden chain "laid down" a decree, when that lesson carefully avoids the word. This lesson is where the golden chain gets applied ("Cash out The Golden Chain here"), so this is the exact spot where the decretal frame slips back in. Severity: high.
- **sections/04-faithfulness/02-potter.md, "He foreknew — fore-loved — these persons as they actually would be"** — "as they actually would be" is foresight language: God knowing beforehand what these people would turn out like. The golden chain insists the opposite: foreknowledge is "not to have advance information about someone" and its object is "*whom*, not *what about them*". The golden chain's own wording is "as we actually were". This quietly turns the relational reading back into an informational one. Severity: low.

## 5. The Jew/Gentile convention is chapter-bound

- **sections/04-faithfulness/07-unsearchable.md, "the calling that cannot be revoked is the effectual call of God to His own"** — This resolves the broken-branches/irrevocable-calling tension by moving the 11:29 calling off national Israel and onto the elect individuals, the invisible church. The section overview says that is exactly what must not happen: substitute the referent and "the irrevocable gifts and calling of 11:29 have nobody to be irrevocable to". In Paul, the same "they" are enemies as regards the gospel and beloved as regards election (11:28). The broken-off branches and the people whose calling is irrevocable are the same nation. The lesson splits them into two groups, so Israel-as-nation stops being the object of 11:29. That is a replacement move, just made through the visible/invisible distinction rather than through the chapter-2 cipher. The lesson also says "the immediate referent stays what it is" and then contradicts it. Relatedly, the lesson never mentions 11:26 ("all Israel will be saved"), which a national-Israel reading of 11:25-36 has to face. Severity: high.
- **sections/04-faithfulness/06-do-not-boast.md, "A branch that can be broken off was really in the tree in some sense — that is the visible church"** — This equates the olive tree, whose root and natural branches are Israel, with "the visible church". The permitted move is Israel as a *worked example* of the visible/invisible distinction. Naming the tree itself as the visible church comes close to equating Israel with the church. Severity: low. Unsure: this may be meant as "the Roman Gentile branches are the visible church", but as written the whole tree is the antecedent.
- **sections/04-faithfulness/03-stumbling-stone.md, "this is sin as defined in The Fall, in its most religious possible form"** — Israel's pursuit of its own righteousness is described in the same terms used for the chapter-2 religious insider ("most religious possible form"). The reflection then turns it straight onto the reader ("my most respectable attempt"). This lesson does not say Israel means churchgoers. But nothing in it marks that Israel here is a nation, and not the religious-insider type the reader was trained to hear in chapters 2-4. That makes this the lesson most likely to re-import the cipher. Severity: low. Unsure, because applying the pattern to the reader is legitimate.

## 6. Perseverance is taught from 8:29-30, not from 11:29

- **sections/04-faithfulness/07-unsearchable.md, "If the calling is irrevocable, what does that change about how I hold my own standing"** — The reflection hangs the reader's own assurance directly on 11:29. That makes 11:29 carry the perseverance doctrine for the individual, after the content has just said it only corroborates. The second half, "and about how I pray for someone who has walked away", invites the reader to apply 11:29 to an individual apostate. That is exactly the universalising the section's teacher note warns against ("Someone will universalise 11:29 into a promise about anyone who once professed faith"). The learner lesson does not guard against it; it prompts it. Severity: high.
- **sections/04-faithfulness/07-unsearchable.md, "the section's load-bearing claim. What God's call establishes, God does not revoke."** — This lifts 11:29 into a general axiom about God's call. Stated this way, it *is* the perseverance doctrine, sourced from 11:29. The "corroborated, not taught" disclaimer comes one bullet later and does not undo the axiom. Severity: medium.

## 2. The study is for one person working alone

- **sections/03-reign/10-golden-chain.md, "Establish this from scripture rather than asserting it."** — This is an instruction to the person presenting or writing the lesson, not to the reader. A reader cannot "assert" or "establish" anything to anyone. It reads as a leftover teaching directive. Severity: medium.
- **sections/03-reign/10-golden-chain.md, "This is the lesson's centre of gravity, and it takes real time."** — This is pacing advice for whoever runs the session: spend your time here. It is not addressed to the reader's own reading. Severity: low.
- **sections/04-faithfulness/07-unsearchable.md, "Hold the apparent contradiction and resolve it, do not dodge it."** and **"Set this beside The Potter deliberately."** — Both are presenter directions. "Do not dodge it" warns a teacher tempted to skip a hard point, and "set this beside" is one of the presenter verbs the rule names. They can be read as addressed to the reader, but they are not phrased that way. Severity: low.
- **sections/04-faithfulness/02-potter.md, "Cash out The Golden Chain here."** — An imperative to the lesson's deliverer ("cash out" the earlier teaching), not to the reader. Severity: low.
- **sections/01-indictment/06-does-our-failure-undo-god.md, "Handle the objections head-on"** and **"Worth naming for us"** — The aim is written as a teacher's task, and "naming" is presenter-voice. Severity: low.
- **Aims written as the designer's intent: sections/06-errand/02-household.md "Let the names land"; sections/03-reign/11-nothing-separates.md "Let the doctrinal half of the letter land as assurance"; sections/01-indictment/04-judge-in-the-dock.md "Close the escape hatch"; sections/03-reign/09-all-things-work-together.md "Give one verse a whole lesson"; sections/01-indictment/01-doulos-and-the-gospel.md "Put the study's governing words on the table"** — "Let X land" describes an effect on an audience, and several aims describe what the lesson designer is doing rather than what the reader will do. Severity: low. This is a pattern, not a single slip.
- **overview.md, "# Romans: Study Pacing"** — "Pacing" is course-delivery language. The document is a reader's orientation, not a schedule. Severity: low. Unsure: no meeting is implied beyond the title.
- **sections/01-indictment/02-honor-him-as-god.md, "operating on this week"**, **06-do-not-boast.md "in me this week"**, **07-whatever-is-not-from-faith.md "doing this week"** — "This week" could hint at a weekly meeting cadence. Read in context, it is the reader's own time frame. Severity: low. Unsure; listed only because the rule names "week".

## 9. The study's own theological framework is applied consistently

- **sections/01-indictment/02-honor-him-as-god.md, "honoring God as God is the *prerequisite* to righteousness"** and **"To be righteous is to reclaim the post we abandoned"** — Both make righteousness something reached by what we do: submission first, then righteousness, and "reclaiming" the post ourselves. Lessons 8-11 then teach righteousness as a gift counted "not earned", with faith as "the empty hand" that "contributes nothing". The study never connects its "ought-ness" definition (being what we were made to be) with righteousness that is *credited* to someone who is not yet that. So a reader holding the through-line as stated will read 3:21-4:25 as a change of definition, or read lesson 2 as works-righteousness. Severity: medium.
- **sections/03-reign/09-all-things-work-together.md, "you submit to Him entirely, as Lord over what you want"** set against **sections/02-purchase/04-faith-that-receives.md, "Faith as the empty hand: it contributes nothing and receives everything"** — Lesson 22 builds full submission into *fiducia* and credits lesson 11 as its source. But lesson 11 defines *fiducia* only as resting, and calls faith purely receptive. Neither lesson reconciles the two. Severity: low. Probably reconcilable, but the study does not do it.

## 1. Every study stands alone

- **overview.md, "`outline.md` maps what Paul is *arguing*; this maps the work"** — The published study overview points the reader to a design aid that is not part of the published study. A reader of the PDF or site has no `outline.md`. Severity: medium.
- **overview.md, "Those blocks appear below only where the reading is already decided; the rest are filled in as we work."** — This is the author's workshop status leaking into the reader's orientation. "As we work" is the author's collective, not the reader's. "Appear below" is also wrong, because the lessons are no longer below the overview. Severity: medium.
- **sections/04-faithfulness/07-unsearchable.md, "the effectual call of God to His own"** — "Effectual call" is technical Reformed vocabulary that the study never introduces or defines. The lesson's whole resolution rests on it, and a reader without the systematic-theology background cannot follow the move. Severity: medium.
- **overview.md, "the unregenerate visible church versus the world"** — "Visible church" is used as a working term from the opening, but its contrast, the invisible church, is only explained in the Part Four overview and lesson 25. In chapters 2-4 the reader gets the term with no definition. Severity: low.

## 10. Internal coherence

- **overview.md, "chapters 1-11 carry more content than reflection, chapters 12-16 invert that"** — This is false as written. In chapters 12-16 the reflection sections are mostly a single question against three to six content bullets. Examples: Sober Judgment has 3 content and 1 reflection, Love Fulfils the Law 5/1, Bear with the Failings 5/1, Paul's Errand 5/1, The Household 4/1. The promised inversion does not exist. Severity: medium.
- **sections/03-reign/11-nothing-separates.md, "Let the doctrinal half of the letter land as assurance before the turn to Israel"** set against **sections/04-faithfulness/07-unsearchable.md, "End the doctrinal half where Paul ends it"** — The two lessons disagree about where the doctrinal half ends: at 8:39 or at 11:36. Severity: low.
- **overview.md, "this maps the work, lesson by lesson"** — The overview no longer contains any lesson-by-lesson map. That content now lives in the per-lesson files. Severity: low.

## 4. No embedded scripture in bulk

- **sections/03-reign/09-all-things-work-together.md, "casting all your anxieties on Him, because He cares for you"** — This is essentially the whole of 1 Peter 5:7 in ESV wording, not a phrase inside the author's prose. Severity: medium.
- **sections/04-faithfulness/02-potter.md, "God shows His love toward us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died"** — This is nearly all of Romans 5:8 in ESV wording. The golden chain's cross-reference ("He demonstrates His love toward us in that while we were still sinners Christ died for us") gives the whole verse a second time. Severity: medium.
- **sections/05-ethics/06-to-his-own-master-he-stands.md, "Who are you to pass judgment on the servant of another?"** — Together with "He stands or falls to his own master", this reproduces most of Romans 14:4. Severity: low.
- **Cross-reference glosses in sections/03-reign/09-all-things-work-together.md and 10-golden-chain.md: "you meant evil against me, but God meant it for good", "no purpose of Yours can be thwarted", "you only have I known of all the families of the earth", "before I formed you in the womb I knew you"** — Each is a half-verse or more in ESV wording. Each one alone is a phrase, but together they make the cross-reference blocks read as a verse anthology. Severity: low. Unsure whether this crosses the line.
- **Content bullets that walk the passage clause by clause, e.g. sections/03-reign/11-nothing-separates.md, "If God is for us, who can be against us?"** — Several lessons (Nothing Separates, Died to Sin, Bear with the Failings) build their content mostly from successive clauses of the passage in near-ESV wording. Severity: low. This is a pattern worth watching, not a clear breach.

## 3. Teacher notes are segregated

- **sections/04-faithfulness/07-unsearchable.md, "Take it as supporting evidence rather than as the proof-text"** and **"so it is clear which passage carries the doctrine and which illustrates it"** — This is the author's methodological guardrail about which text grounds which doctrine. It reads as a note to someone preparing the lesson, not as something the solo reader needs to do. Severity: low.
- **sections/03-reign/10-golden-chain.teacher.md, "*How can God love us before we exist?*"** — A reader working alone will raise this objection, and the only answer ("The persons are determinate to Him before they are actual to us") is in the teacher file. The learner lesson gestures at it through Jeremiah 1:5 but never answers it. Severity: low. Unsure, because the rule does place anticipated objections in teacher notes.
